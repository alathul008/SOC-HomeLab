# Incident Reports

Professional incident reports turn technical investigation into an analyst-ready deliverable.

## Report Standard

Each report should include:

### 1. Executive Summary
Short description of the incident and business/security impact.

### 2. Detection
What generated the alert and which telemetry source was involved.

### 3. Timeline
Chronological sequence of relevant events.

### 4. Evidence
Relevant sanitized log events, screenshots, queries, and indicators.

### 5. Analysis
Reasoning used to distinguish malicious behavior from benign activity.

### 6. MITRE ATT&CK
Techniques supported by the observed evidence.

### 7. Severity & Impact
Risk rating, affected assets, and potential consequences.

### 8. Response
Containment, eradication, recovery, and validation steps appropriate to the lab scenario.

### 9. Lessons Learned
Detection gaps and improvements to reduce recurrence.

All reports are based on controlled lab activity and sanitized evidence.
